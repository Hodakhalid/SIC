About GradeFlow

GradeWise is a simple and user-friendly web application designed
to help students calculate their grades quickly and accurately. By
entering the required information, students can instantly determine
their academic grade, understand their performance, and stay
motivated to achieve better results.

Our goal is to make grade calculation easy, fast, and accessible
for every student.
